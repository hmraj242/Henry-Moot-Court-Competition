# Count 2 Prosecution: cue sheet (practise from this, not the full script)

1. **Test (Art 52(2)):** both effective contribution AND definite advantage; if either missing, civilian.
2. **Necessity:** once civilian, necessity cannot justify attack (Kordić and Čerkez).
3. **Schools (Art 52(3)):** doubt about use means presumed civilian; President's own doubt (SAF ¶42).
4. **No use:** no positive action (SAF ¶42); Thorn: "Even if they are not being used now"; the twelve had recruits and weapons, these three nothing.
5. **Dismissal of pupils:** NO, Art 58 precaution, near railway, 17 struck, no military step.
6. **Location:** Cabinet chose schools near railway (SAF ¶37): suitability, not contribution. (No hospital/stadium example: teacher says it is not a legal argument.)
7. **Purpose:** four routes (nature no, location no, use no) so only purpose left. Purpose = future use; contribution must be effective, ICRC: actual situation not abstract, so concrete and tied to this school. Twenty-school category; twelve converted and in use (military, conceded); five only after strikes; last three: decisions not yet made = possibility, not purpose. Do not use "any building" argument; do not state Hirgigo facts (principle only).
   - If asked "fit for purpose" (SAF ¶37): Cabinet did intend to use schools of that type; phrase describes features; a plan for a category is not a concrete purpose for each school.
8. **Advantage:** advantage lies in the future, not "in the circumstances ruling at the time".
